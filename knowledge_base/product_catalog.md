# Product Catalogue

This catalogue describes every product the store carries, grouped by
category. Descriptions, features, and warranty terms live here; the live
price and stock amount for a product always come from the product
information lookup at the time of the question, never from this document.

## Audio category

The audio category covers headphones and speakers. Audio products carry a
24-month manufacturer warranty.

### P001 — Aurora Wireless Headphones

Over-ear wireless headphones with adaptive noise cancellation, 40-hour
battery life, and multipoint connection to two devices. Includes a hard
carry case and a USB-C fast cable. A full charge takes 90 minutes. The
warranty covers battery defects. In-ear hygiene tips are not covered once
opened. Current price and stock are confirmed live from the product
catalogue records.

### P002 — Nimbus Smart Speaker

Voice-assistant smart speaker with room-filling 360-degree sound, Wi-Fi and
Bluetooth pairing, and multi-room grouping with other Nimbus units. Supports
the store's app for firmware updates. Fabric grille available in light grey
and sand. 12-month warranty on electronics.

## Computers category

The computers category covers monitors and desktop peripherals. Computer
products carry a 36-month warranty.

### P003 — Zenith 4K Monitor

27-inch 4K IPS monitor with 99% sRGB coverage, a height-adjustable stand,
and two HDMI 2.1 ports plus one DisplayPort. Ships factory-calibrated with a
calibration report in the box. Dead-pixel policy: up to five dead pixels
within the warranty period qualifies for a replacement. Current price and
stock are confirmed live from the product catalogue records.

## Wearables category

### P004 — Pulse Fitness Watch

GPS fitness watch with heart-rate and sleep tracking, 10-day battery life,
5 ATM water resistance, and interchangeable straps. Syncs health data to
the store's app on both phone platforms. Strap sizes S/M and L are
included. 12-month warranty excluding strap wear.

## Home category

### P005 — Lumen Desk Lamp

LED desk lamp with stepless dimming, three colour temperatures, a memory
function, and a USB-A charging port in the base. Aluminum body with a
weighted base for stability. 24-month warranty. Current price and stock are
confirmed live from the product catalogue records.

### P006 — Breeze Air Purifier

Room air purifier covering up to 40 square metres with a HEPA H13 filter,
auto mode using a laser particle sensor, and a night mode under 25 dB.
Filter replacement is recommended every 6 to 8 months; replacement filters
are ordered at the counter. 24-month warranty on the unit, filters are
consumables.

## Accessories category

### P007 — Vertex Mechanical Keyboard

Hot-swappable mechanical keyboard with a 75% layout, gasket mounting, RGB
backlighting, and tri-mode connection (USB-C, Bluetooth 5.1, 2.4 GHz).
Ships with the store's linear switches installed plus a puller and spare
keycaps. Engraving is available, and engraved units are non-refundable.
12-month warranty.

### P008 — Halo Smartphone Stand

Aluminum smartphone stand with an adjustable angle, silicone contact pads,
and a folded footprint that fits a pocket. Holds devices up to 7 inches in
portrait or landscape. 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects.

## Product questions and purchases

For any product question, retrieve this catalogue for the description and
features, then confirm the live price and stock through the product
information lookup before quoting anything. A purchase requires the product
id, the quantity, enough stock, and enough account balance; the purchase
tools check all three before executing. After a purchase, the receipt
summary shows the item, quantity, total price, and the new balance.
