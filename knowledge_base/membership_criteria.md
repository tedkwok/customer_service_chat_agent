# Membership Criteria and Benefits

Every registered account starts at the **Basic** level and can progress
through **Silver**, **Gold**, and **Platinum**. Eligibility is always
verified from the customer's live records, never from what the customer
remembers. The registration procedure at the end of this document describes
exactly how a tier change is performed.

## How progress is measured

Two numbers decide eligibility for every tier.

1. **Qualifying spend.** The total value of completed purchases in the last
   12 months. Purchases made with account balance count in full. Refunded
   purchases are subtracted once the refund completes.
2. **Account age.** The number of days since the account was created.

Both numbers come from the customer record and the purchase history for the
last 12 months; the history covers purchase dates, item names, categories,
prices, and quantities.

## Basic tier

Basic is the entry level granted automatically when an account is created.
There is no spend or age requirement. Basic members earn a 20.00 birthday
voucher each year and pay the standard delivery fee. Delivery is free for
orders over 300.00.

## Silver tier

A customer qualifies for Silver when qualifying spend reaches 3,000.00 or
more and the account is at least 60 days old. Silver members enjoy a 5%
discount on every purchase, free delivery on all orders, and priority
support through the dedicated chat queue.

## Gold tier

A customer qualifies for Gold when qualifying spend reaches 10,000.00 or
more and the account is at least 180 days old. Gold members enjoy a 10%
discount on every purchase, free express delivery, an extended 45-day
refund window on eligible products, and access to the members-only hotline.

## Platinum tier

A customer qualifies for Platinum when qualifying spend reaches 30,000.00 or
more and the account is at least 365 days old. Platinum members enjoy a 15%
discount on every purchase, free express delivery, an annual 500.00 voucher
credited on the membership anniversary, and a personal shopping assistant.

## Tier retention and downgrade

Tiers are re-evaluated every membership anniversary. If the qualifying
spend of the last 12 months falls below the tier threshold, the customer is
moved down one tier, never more than one tier per year. Customers are
notified 30 days before any downgrade.

## Membership registration procedure

A tier upgrade is executed only after eligibility is proven from live
records, using this exact procedure.

1. Check the customer's current membership level, account creation date, and
   account balance through the customer information lookup.
2. Retrieve the customer's purchase history for the last 12 months and
   total the spend.
3. Compare spend and account age against the tier thresholds in this
   document. If either condition fails, tell the customer exactly which
   condition is unmet and by how much.
4. If both conditions pass, confirm the target tier with the customer, then
   register the new membership level for the customer.
5. Report the new level back and remind the customer that the benefits take
   effect immediately.

Tier changes are free of charge. Customers may never pay to skip a tier.
