# Refund Policy

This document defines when a purchase can be refunded and how a refund
request proceeds. Refund decisions always require human approval before any
money moves. A refund request is always tied to one specific purchase record
id, which must be matched against the customer's purchase history.

## Refund eligibility window

A purchase is eligible for refund within 30 days of the purchase date.
Gold and Platinum members enjoy an extended window of 45 days for eligible
product categories. The purchase date on the customer's purchase history
record is the sole source of truth for the deadline; the day of purchase
itself counts as day one.

## Item condition requirements

To be refunded, an item must be unused and in its original packaging with
all accessories, manuals, and free gifts included. Items that arrive
damaged, faulty, or inconsistent with the ordered product are always
eligible regardless of condition, because the fault is the store's.

## Non-refundable items

The following cannot be refunded under any circumstances.

1. Clearance items marked final sale at purchase time.
2. Personalized or engraved items, including engraved accessories.
3. Digital gift cards and redeemed voucher codes.
4. Opened hygiene-sensitive products such as in-ear headphone tips.

## Refund process

The refund process runs through these steps in order.

1. The customer states which purchase they want refunded. The purchase
   record id must be matched against the customer's purchase history; an id
   recited from memory is never accepted without this match.
2. Eligibility is checked against the window, the item condition, and the
   exclusion list in this document.
3. The refund request is submitted for the purchase record id, which emails
   the request to the store manager.
4. A human manager reviews the request and approves or rejects it. The
   agent must relay the approval question to the customer exactly as asked
   and wait for an explicit agree or disagree reply.
5. Once approved, the refund executes.

A refund decision takes up to 3 business days. Refunds are never executed
before the approval step completes.

## Refund method and timeline

After approval, refunds are credited to the customer's account balance
within 24 hours. A customer who paid by card may instead request the refund
on the original card, which takes 5 to 7 business days. Original delivery
fees are refunded only when the item was faulty or the wrong item was sent.

## Partial refunds and quantity

Multi-quantity purchases may be refunded in part; the refund amount covers
only the returned units at the recorded purchase price. Promotional
discounts applied at purchase time are deducted proportionally from the
refund amount.

## Failed or refused refunds

When a refund request is refused, the customer must be told which rule in
this document blocked it and offered the nearest allowed alternative, such
as an exchange or store credit where policy permits. A refused refund can
be escalated to a human manager on request.
