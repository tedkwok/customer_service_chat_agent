# Store General Policy

Welcome to Meridian Store. This document collects the general operating rules
of the store. Questions about a specific customer's balance, history, or
transactions are handled by the operation agent, not by this document.

## Operating hours and channels

The store serves customers through the website, the mobile app, and the
downtown flagship counter. Online support operates every day from 09:00 to
21:00 local time. Orders placed online are processed the next business
morning. The flagship counter is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 19:00
and is closed on public holidays.

## Payment methods

Customers may pay with any of the following methods.

1. **Account balance.** Every registered customer has a store account
   balance. The balance can be topped up on the account page and spent on
   any purchase. Balance is held in store credit and has no expiry date.
2. **Card payment.** Major credit and debit cards are accepted at the
   flagship counter.

When a customer pays with account balance, the purchase amount is deducted
atomically at the moment the purchase is confirmed. If the balance is
insufficient, the purchase is refused and the customer should be invited to
top up first.

## Account balance rules

Account balance is personal to the customer and is not transferable between
accounts. The minimum single top-up amount is 10.00. Refunds for eligible
purchases are credited to the account balance within 24 hours of approval
unless the customer asks for the original payment method instead. Balance
amounts are always confirmed live through the customer information tool, and
support staff must never quote a balance from memory.

## Pricing and price matching

All prices are set by the store and are confirmed live from the product
catalogue at the time of purchase. If a product's price drops within 14 days
of a customer's purchase, the customer may request a one-time price
adjustment for the difference, credited to the account balance. Price
adjustments require the original purchase record.

## Order changes and cancellation

An order may be cancelled free of charge any time before it ships. Once an
order has shipped, the customer must use the refund process instead.
Address changes are possible before shipment by contacting support with the
purchase record id.

## Gift wrapping and services

Gift wrapping is available for 15.00 per item at the flagship counter and
for online orders at checkout. Personalized engraving is available for
accessories and makes those items non-refundable.

## Escalation and complaints

If a customer is not satisfied with the outcome of a request, they may ask
for the case to be escalated to a human manager. Refund decisions are always
reviewed by a human manager before they take effect. Complaints are answered
within two business days.

## Customer conduct and privacy

Customers should never be asked to reveal their internal customer
identifier; support identifies the account from the session. Personal data
is used only for order handling and membership administration and is never
shared with third parties.
